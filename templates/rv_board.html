How this board is built
The fit

Each session a smooth curve a + b·√dte + c·dte is fitted through the live generics plus spot VIX pinned at zero days at double weight. The spot anchor is load-bearing: without it the fit drifts at the front, where the interesting dislocations live, and hit rate falls about 14 points.

A structure's deviation is the weighted sum of its legs' residuals — which is what a fly or a ratio measures by construction.

Why the seasonal offsets

Every contract month carries a persistent level against any smooth curve, because the calendar period it covers holds a persistent amount of realised volatility. December has printed below a plain fit in 98% of sessions and in every year since 2013; October is the mirror image.

Left uncorrected that is a permanent false signal, and the offending contract also drags the fitted curve toward itself, making its neighbours look mispriced. So the offsets are estimated causally — median of prior residuals for that month — and removed from the prices before the fit.

Reading the panels

Solid is where a structure actually trades, dashed is what the fitted curve implies. Because the fitted level carries each offset back, actual − fitted is the adjusted deviation exactly — the vertical gap is the signal.

Only consecutive-leg ladders appear. Wide calendars and skip-flies share a family with different leg spacings, so plotting them on one axis would imply a sequence that does not exist; they sit in the carry book.

Trigger and bracket

A single rolling-252 z-score of the adjusted deviation at |z| ≥ 1.5, traded against it. The month z-score is shown for transparency and gates nothing — once seasonality is removed at contract level, confirming against a month baseline corrects for it twice.

Target 2σ, stop 1σ of the structure's own trailing daily move, 15-session time stop. Fixed tick targets underperform because σ runs from 10 to 92 ticks across the curve.

Costs and units

One tick is 0.01 index points of the weighted sum — $10 on one structure unit at the VX $1,000 multiplier. Same 100× convention as the existing engine.

Commission is $2.50 per contract per round trip: $5 / $10 / $15 / $25 for a spread / fly / 1:2 / 2:3. Calibrated expectancies add one further tick of slippage.

Limits worth remembering

Barriers are checked on settlements, so an overnight gap can carry an exit past the stop. Expectancies come from one 13.6-year sample with thresholds chosen by search — the variance-ratio evidence underneath is parameter-free, the per-trade numbers are optimistically biased.

2013–2017 was materially weaker than 2018 onward. Judge structures on incremental edge over an always-sell baseline, never on raw annual ticks.
